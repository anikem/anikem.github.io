Connector architectures
  1. Linear / MLP (LLaVA)
    Simplest; remarkably effective.
  2. Cross-attention (Flamingo, Llama 3.2V)
    Adds cross-attention layers into the LLM.
  3. Q-Former (BLIP-2)
    Learned queries compress visual features to 32 tokens.
  4. Pixel Shuffle (InternVL)
    Spatially merge adjacent patches (4× fewer tokens).

  1. Start with a trained LLM and a trained Vision Encoder.
  2. Pre-train the VLM to teach it multimodal alignment.
  3. Post-train on downstream tasks.

Reinforcement Learning
From RLHF to GRPO
  • RLHF aligned LLMs to human intentA reward model trained on human preferences, then PPO — the recipe behind ChatGPT. (Ouyang et al., 2022)
  • DPO dropped the reward modelOptimize the preference objective directly; simple and widely used for VLM alignment. (Rafailov et al., 2023)
  • GRPO is now the popular defaultGroup-relative baseline, no critic network — cheaper than PPO, more stable than DPO. (Shao et al. / DeepSeek, 2024)
  • Adopted across frontier models

The Eight BitterBetter Data Lessons
Lesson 1
Quality beats Quantity
High quality data and annotations serve as a data and compute multiplier.
Lesson 2
Spend time on annotation design
Molmo's speech-based captioning hugely improved data quality.
Count-by-pointing turned annotation into chain-of-thought.
Lesson 3
Use AI models to push your data frontier
Human annotations are best used in conjunction with SOTA models.
Lesson 4
Synthetic data can shine
Particularly when human data is hard/impossible to obtain and scale.
But take care to ensure it's not OOD.
Lesson 5
Mixing and curriculum matter
One can often spend as much effort gathering SFT data as curating pre-training data in spite of it being orders of magnitude smaller.
Lesson 6
Architecture decisions are data decisions
Carefully consider the implications of your architecture and training choices on your data choices and vice versa.
Lesson 7
Filtering and curation are evolving arts
Seemingly simple tasks like filtering and deduplication can have outsized impacts on model performance.
Lesson 8
You cannot run away from
annotator dynamics
Treat your annotator pool with respect. Pay them well, make their work interesting and they will give you back returns on your investment.
